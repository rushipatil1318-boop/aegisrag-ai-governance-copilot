# Data and data-governance requirements

## Governance of datasets
High-risk AI systems that are trained with data must use training, validation and testing datasets that meet quality criteria. The datasets are subject to data-governance and management practices suited to the intended purpose.

## What the practices cover
These practices cover design choices, data-collection processes and the origin of data, the original purpose of collection for personal data, preparation steps such as annotation, labelling, cleaning and aggregation, the assumptions the data is meant to represent, the availability, quantity and suitability of data, the examination of possible biases that could affect health, safety or fundamental rights, measures to detect, prevent and mitigate those biases, and the identification of data gaps.

## Quality of the data
Datasets must be relevant, sufficiently representative and, to the best extent possible, free of errors and complete for the intended purpose, and must take into account the specific geographical, behavioural or functional setting. Special categories of personal data may be processed only exceptionally, with strict safeguards, where this is necessary to detect and correct bias.
