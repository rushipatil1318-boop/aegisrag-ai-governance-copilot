# EU Artificial Intelligence Act — risk-based framework overview

## Purpose and scope
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 sets harmonised rules for placing on the market, putting into service and using AI systems in the European Union. It applies to providers and deployers inside the EU and to providers outside the EU when the output of their system is used in the Union.

## Risk-based structure
The Act sorts AI uses into tiers. Certain practices are prohibited outright. High-risk AI systems must meet requirements on risk management, data governance, documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity. Some systems that interact with people or generate content carry transparency duties. Other systems face no specific obligations, although voluntary codes of conduct are encouraged. General-purpose AI models have their own set of obligations.

## Timeline
The Regulation entered into force on 1 August 2024 and applies in stages. Prohibitions applied first, then obligations for general-purpose AI models, and then the high-risk requirements. Always check the current consolidated text, because the application dates for some obligations have been the subject of amendment proposals.
