# Transparency to deployers and human oversight

## Instructions for use
High-risk AI systems must be transparent enough for deployers to interpret their output and use them appropriately. They come with instructions for use that state the provider's identity, the intended purpose, the level of accuracy and its metrics, known limitations and circumstances that may lead to risks, the human-oversight measures, the expected lifetime and the logging mechanisms.

## Human oversight
High-risk AI systems must be designed so that natural persons can oversee them effectively while they are in use. The people assigned to oversight should be able to understand the capacities and limitations of the system and monitor its operation, remain aware of the tendency to over-rely on its output (automation bias), correctly interpret the output, decide not to use the system or to disregard, override or reverse its output, and intervene or stop the system through a stop button or a similar procedure.
