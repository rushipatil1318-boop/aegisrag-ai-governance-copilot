# NIST AI RMF MAP function

## Purpose
MAP establishes the context in which an AI system will operate so that risks related to that context can be identified. The information gathered in MAP informs decisions about whether and how to proceed.

## What it includes
The intended purposes, potentially beneficial uses, context-specific laws, norms and expectations, and the prospective deployment settings are understood and documented. The AI system is categorised, and its capabilities, targeted usage, goals and expected benefits and costs are compared with appropriate benchmarks. Risks and benefits are mapped for all components of the system, including third-party software and data. Likely impacts on individuals, groups, communities, organisations and society are characterised.
