# NIST AI RMF MANAGE function

## Purpose
MANAGE allocates risk resources to the mapped and measured risks on a regular basis, as defined by GOVERN. It covers the plans to respond to, recover from and communicate about incidents or events.

## What it includes
AI risks identified through MAP and MEASURE are prioritised and responded to based on their projected impact. Responses can mitigate, transfer, avoid or accept a risk. Strategies to maximise benefits and minimise negative impacts are planned, prepared, implemented and documented, including mechanisms to supersede, disengage or deactivate systems that perform inconsistently with their intended use. Risks from third-party resources are monitored. Risk treatments, including response and recovery plans, are documented and monitored regularly, and incidents and errors are communicated to relevant AI actors and affected communities.
