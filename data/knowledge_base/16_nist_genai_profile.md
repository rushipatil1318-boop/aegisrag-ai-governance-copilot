# NIST Generative AI Profile

## Purpose
The Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, July 2024) is a companion resource to the AI RMF. It helps organisations identify risks that are unique to or made worse by generative AI and suggests actions organised by the GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE functions.

## Risks it names
The profile lists twelve risks: CBRN information or capabilities; confabulation, where a model confidently produces false content; dangerous, violent or hateful content; data privacy; environmental impacts; harmful bias and homogenisation; human-AI configuration, such as over-reliance; information integrity; information security, including prompt injection and data poisoning; intellectual property; obscene, degrading or abusive content; and value-chain and component integration.

## Suggested practices
Suggested actions include pre-deployment testing and red-teaming, evaluating retrieval and grounding quality, tracking content provenance, monitoring after deployment, and having incident-disclosure and response processes.
