# EU AI Act high-risk classification

## Two routes to high-risk
An AI system is high-risk when it is a safety component of a product, or is itself a product, covered by the Union harmonisation legislation in Annex I that requires third-party conformity assessment. It is also high-risk when it is used in one of the areas listed in Annex III.

## Annex III areas
Annex III covers biometrics, critical infrastructure, education and vocational training, employment and worker management, access to essential private and public services, law enforcement, migration, asylum and border control, and the administration of justice and democratic processes. For employment this includes systems used to recruit or select people, for example to place targeted job advertisements, analyse and filter applications and evaluate or rank candidates, and systems used to decide on promotion, termination, task allocation or to monitor performance.

## Narrow exception
An Annex III system is not high-risk if it does not pose a significant risk of harm, for example when it only performs a narrow procedural task or a preparatory task. This exception never applies when the system profiles natural persons. A provider who relies on it must document the assessment.
