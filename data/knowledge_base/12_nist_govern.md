# NIST AI RMF GOVERN function

## Purpose
GOVERN cultivates a culture of risk management in organisations that design, develop, deploy or use AI systems. It is cross-cutting and applies to all stages of the other functions.

## What it includes
Policies, processes, procedures and practices for mapping, measuring and managing AI risks are in place, transparent and implemented effectively. Legal and regulatory requirements are understood and managed. Accountability structures make sure the right teams and individuals are empowered, responsible and trained. Workforce diversity, equity, inclusion and accessibility are prioritised. Teams are committed to a culture that considers and communicates AI risk. Processes exist for engagement with relevant AI actors and affected communities, and for addressing risks that come from third-party software, data and supply chains.
