# Technical documentation and record-keeping

## Technical documentation
Before a high-risk AI system is placed on the market or put into service, the provider must draw up technical documentation and keep it up to date. Annex IV lists the content: a general description of the system and its intended purpose, a description of its elements and development process, information on monitoring, functioning and control, the risk-management system, changes over the lifecycle, the standards applied and the post-market monitoring plan. The documentation shows authorities that the system meets the requirements.

## Automatic logs
High-risk AI systems must technically allow automatic recording of events, or logs, over their lifetime. Logging gives a level of traceability suited to the intended purpose. Logs help to identify situations that may result in a risk or a substantial modification, support post-market monitoring, and allow deployers to monitor the operation of the system.
