# Risk-management system for high-risk AI

## A continuous process
Providers of high-risk AI systems must establish, implement, document and maintain a risk-management system. It is a continuous, iterative process that runs through the entire lifecycle of the system and is regularly reviewed and updated.

## Steps
The process identifies and analyses known and reasonably foreseeable risks to health, safety and fundamental rights when the system is used as intended. It estimates and evaluates risks that may arise from intended use and from reasonably foreseeable misuse, evaluates further risks based on data from post-market monitoring, and adopts appropriate and targeted risk-management measures.

## Residual risk and testing
Measures should eliminate or reduce risks as far as technically feasible, and the remaining residual risk must be judged acceptable. High-risk systems must be tested against metrics and probabilistic thresholds defined in advance, including where appropriate in real-world conditions. The provider must consider whether the system is likely to affect people under 18 or other vulnerable groups.
