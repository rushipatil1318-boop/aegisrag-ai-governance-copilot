# Deployer obligations and fundamental-rights impact assessment

## Obligations of deployers
Deployers of high-risk AI systems must use them in line with the instructions for use and assign human oversight to people who have the necessary competence, training, authority and support. Where they control input data, they must make sure it is relevant and sufficiently representative. They monitor the operation of the system, inform the provider and authorities about risks and serious incidents, suspend use where needed, and keep automatically generated logs for at least six months.

## Workers and affected persons
Before using a high-risk system at the workplace, employers inform workers' representatives and the affected workers. Deployers that make or assist decisions about natural persons with an Annex III system inform those people that they are subject to such a system.

## Fundamental-rights impact assessment
Bodies governed by public law, private entities providing public services, and deployers of certain credit-scoring and life or health insurance pricing systems must carry out a fundamental-rights impact assessment before first use. It describes the process, the period and frequency of use, the categories of people affected, the specific risks of harm, the human-oversight measures and the measures to take if risks materialise.
