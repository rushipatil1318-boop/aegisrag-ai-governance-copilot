# Data Card

## Dataset purpose

The project corpus supports demonstrations of evidence-grounded AI governance question answering and preliminary AI-system risk screening.

## Composition

- 10 paraphrased reference notes linked to Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and European Commission material.
- 6 paraphrased reference notes linked to NIST AI RMF 1.0 and the NIST Generative AI Profile.
- 10 retrieval evaluation questions with expected source identifiers and answer concepts.
- 3 fictional but realistic AI-system profiles for recruitment, customer support, and clinical triage.

## Provenance

Each knowledge document contains a stable source identifier, title, framework, reference, official URL, topic labels, and last-verification date. The repository stores concise paraphrases, not a replacement copy of the complete legal or framework documents.

## Intended use

- RAG engineering demonstration
- Retrieval and citation evaluation
- Responsible-AI workflow prototyping
- Education and portfolio review

## Out-of-scope use

- Legal advice
- Automated conformity assessment
- Final prohibited-practice or high-risk classification
- Decisions about individual rights, employment, healthcare, credit, or public services

## Maintenance

Before production use, an accountable reviewer should re-check official URLs, amendments, guidance, application dates, and source summaries. Evaluation data should be expanded with subject-matter-expert labels and adversarial examples.
